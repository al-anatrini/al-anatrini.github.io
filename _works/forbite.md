<div class="work-section section-dark">
  <h2 class="section-title">Concept</h2>

  <div class="concept-text">
    <p>Commissioned by IRCAM's Académie ManiFeste 2015 and dedicated to Paul Riveaux, Forbite fantascienze—"Forbidden Science Fictions"—explores contrabassoon's timbral extremes through real-time electronic augmentation. Extended techniques were systematically explored, recorded, and analyzed during intensive residency collaboration.</p>

    <div class="highlight-box">
      The contrabassoon occupies liminal acoustic space: too low for melodic clarity, too present for pure bass function. The work embraces this ambiguity, treating the instrument as generator of sub-bass rumbles, visceral air noise, and spectral shadows.
    </div>

    <p>Electronics function as both mirror and distortion lens. Microphone captures acoustic signal, processed through time-domain and frequency-domain transformations. The 8.1 spatial configuration distributes processed sounds across horizontal plane and subwoofer, creating immersive low-frequency environment extending into infrasonic territory. Formal structure derives from timbral transformation rather than pitch development.</p>
  </div>
</div>
