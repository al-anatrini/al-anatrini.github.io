<div class="work-section section-dark">
  <h2 class="section-title">Concept</h2>

  <div class="concept-text">
    <p>Commissioned for Hamburg Dialogues 2019 and dedicated to Nicolas Haumann and Martin Donoso Vera, Tap This explores electric guitar as melodic instrument and percussive interface. The title references two-handed tapping—both hands striking fretboard, generating rapid sequences impossible through conventional plucking.</p>

    <div class="highlight-box">
      The work challenges the electric guitar's cultural associations. Neither rock virtuosity nor contemporary abstraction, it seeks a third space where technical display serves compositional structure rather than spectacle.
    </div>

    <p>Extended techniques—harmonic tapping, percussive strikes, prepared strings, feedback manipulation—transform guitars into hybrid electroacoustic instruments. Timbral exploration drives formal architecture: clean tones with chorus, overdriven distortion, tremolo-picked shimmer, prepared metallic resonances. The relationship between two guitars remains deliberately ambiguous—tight synchronization, independent trajectories, single composite instrument.</p>
  </div>
</div>
