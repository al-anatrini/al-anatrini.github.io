<div class="work-section section-dark">
  <h2 class="section-title">Concept</h2>

  <div class="concept-text">
    <p>A cultivated place appears where it should not, and perhaps for that very reason, for a moment, it seems necessary. An ancient yet restrained green: leaves, tendrils and blossoms follow the rhythm of the façade, making it feel inhabited.</p>

    <p>Bròlo reflects on the visual and acoustic formalisation of nature, and on the historical layers embedded in museum interfaces. A public surface, a threshold between inside and outside, is crossed by a remote logic, revealing how we shape nature by turning it into image, model, surface and shared experience.</p>
