<div class="work-section section-dark">
  <h2 class="section-title">Concept</h2>

  <div class="concept-text">
    <p>Commissioned by Ensemble Musiques Nouvelles for the Tactus Young Composer Forum, Esplorazione di una certa quantità d'acqua transforms acoustic properties of water-filled glass into orchestral material. A goblet containing measured water volume becomes generative source for all musical events, its resonances analyzed and orchestrated for nine instruments.</p>

    <div class="highlight-box">
      The piece traces trajectory from clarity to saturation, exploring what happens when acoustic systems approach and exceed resonant limits. Constant drift toward distortion, interference, unbearable density—and its opposite: return to fundamental simplicity.
    </div>

    <p>Formal architecture mirrors physical resonance behavior. Sections build through additive layering, accumulating energy until ensemble approaches saturation—acoustic equivalent of goblet's resonant peak. At critical thresholds, small perturbations trigger dramatic timbral shifts. The work alternates between sustained resonant states and catastrophic collapse into silence or noise.</p>
  </div>
</div>
