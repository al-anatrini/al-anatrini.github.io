Works

My work unfolds at the intersection of sound, image, and code, through generative systems that respond, transform, and adapt to the point of collapse.

I rework forms from the past (ancient cartographies, premodern systems of thought, speculative architectures) to build immersive environments rooted in an imaginary past. Each piece functions as a living system in flux, suspended between abstraction and hyperreality, familiarity and enigma.
